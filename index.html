Our Mission & Impact

Women on Wheels Pakistan is dedicated to empowering women by providing comprehensive mobility training, fostering independence, and promoting their active participation in society. We believe that mobility is a key to unlocking opportunities and breaking barriers.

Our Training Programs

Motorbike Riding

Comprehensive training from basic handling to road safety and traffic rules.

  • Duration: 4 Weeks
  • Eligibility: 18+ years, Valid ID
  • Locations: Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad
Enroll Now

Scooty Riding

Specialized training for scooty users, focusing on urban mobility and safety.

  • Duration: 3 Weeks
  • Eligibility: 16+ years
  • Locations: Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Multan
Enroll Now

Advanced Road Safety

Advanced course for experienced riders, covering defensive driving and emergency maneuvers.

  • Duration: 2 Weeks
  • Eligibility: Licensed Riders
  • Locations: All major cities
